European Micro-Mobility Fleet Operations & Revenue Optimization

Operational analytics and demand forecasting across 12,000 smart bikes in 6 European cities (Copenhagen, Antwerp, Ghent, Amsterdam, Geneva, and Rotterdam) analyzing 2,000,000 trips to optimize station rebalancing and recover lost revenue.

Scope & Scale

12,000 smart bikes deployed across 6 premier European metropolitan areas (Copenhagen, Antwerp, Ghent, Amsterdam, Geneva, and Rotterdam) resulting in 2,000,000 recorded rider trips.

Key Architecture & Methodology

Designed a production MySQL 8.0 data warehouse and a Python spatial ETL pipeline via SQLAlchemy. Developed an XGBoost 24-hour demand model enabling operations managers to preemptively rebalance stations before stockouts occur.

Impact & Measurable Results

Generated €4.37M in total gross fares. Achieved a 37.2% cut in stockouts at morning commute hubs and recovered €119.5K in revenue over two operational quarters.

Executive Deliverable

Final 3-Page Power BI Fleet Operations Scorecard tracking live station net demand, rebalancing van routing queues, battery critical burn rates, and revenue leakage corridors.

MySQL 8.0 · Python 3.10 · XGBoost · Power BI · Advanced DAX · SQLAlchemy
Nordic Sustainability · PostgreSQL & GeoPandas

Swedish Agri-Carbon Sequestration & Soil Telemetry Platform

An environmental telemetry engine synthesizing IoT soil sensors and multispectral spatial analytics across 10,420 agricultural enterprises in Nordic arable zones.

Scope & Scale

10,420 enterprises and 74.16 million hectares monitored across Nordic arable zones and Swedish agricultural counties.

Key Architecture & Methodology

Built a PostgreSQL IoT data warehouse and Python spatial regression pipeline utilizing GeoPandas and Scikit-Learn. Monitored soil organic carbon flux and nitrogen leaching across Nordic farms, powering an executive Power BI sustainability scorecard.

Impact & Measurable Results

Achieved +14.2% optimization in nitrogen fertilizer efficiency via spatial zoning, reducing excess runoff while validating verified soil carbon sequestration for carbon credits.

Executive Deliverable

Crop & Resource Operations Overview and National Sustainability Summary tracking soil carbon intensity, emissions trends, and regional breakdowns across Swedish counties.

PostgreSQL · Python · GeoPandas · Scikit-Learn · Power BI · IoT Telemetry
Municipal Analytics · MySQL 8.0 & XGBoost

NYC Restaurant Health Risk Engine & Sanitation Predictor

A municipal inspection pipeline and machine learning classification engine auditing 25,180 active commercial food service venues across all New York City boroughs to curb foodborne outbreaks.

Scope & Scale

25,180 active food service venues and commercial kitchens audited across all five New York City boroughs (Manhattan, Brooklyn, Queens, Bronx, Staten Island).

Key Architecture & Methodology

Developed a municipal inspection pipeline and machine learning classification engine on MySQL 8.0 and Python. Integrated an XGBoost Classifier and Folium GIS mapping to prioritize routine and targeted inspections to curb foodborne illness outbreaks.

Impact & Measurable Results

Achieved +31.8% improvement in prioritizing critical sanitary hazards, delivering an advance warning window for municipal food safety enforcement teams.

Executive Deliverable

Food Safety Pulse dashboard tracking risk and progress over time, including borough inspection volume, violation severity comparisons, grade distributions, and cuisine risk breakdowns.

MySQL 8.0 · Python · XGBoost · Folium GIS · Power BI · DAX
